How the estimate is built

Water in each food

Water = grams × water content % ÷ 100
Portions → grams = portions × g per portion

Putting it against your day

Total daily need ≈ drinks goal ÷ 0.80
Share = food water ÷ total need × 100

EFSA's dietary reference values note that foods supply roughly 20% of total water intake, with drinks covering the other 80% — so a drinks goal of 68 fl oz implies a total daily need of about 217 fl oz. The water-content percentages are study-based averages and vary by ±1–2 percentage points between sources; for canonical values we recommend the USDA FoodData Central database. Portion weights (a cup of diced watermelon ≈ 152 g, a medium apple ≈ 182 g) are approximations and fully editable.
