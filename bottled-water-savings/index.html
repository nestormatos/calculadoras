How the estimate is built

Consumption & cost

Liters/year = daily L × days
Bottles/year = liters ÷ bottle size (rounded up)
Bottled cost = bottles × price
Jug cost = (liters ÷ jug size) × refill price
Filter cost = upfront + cartridges × price

Footprint avoided

Plastic (kg) = bottles × grams per bottle ÷ 1000
CO₂ (kg) = liters × (166–222 g per liter) ÷ 1000
Savings = bottled cost − alternative cost

The CO₂ figure uses a study-based estimate of 83–111 g of CO₂ per 0.5 L single-use bottle (roughly 166–222 g per liter, covering production and transport). A returnable jug is refilled, so it adds no new plastic per refill. Tap-water CO₂ is treated as zero — it is small and depends on your city's water system. Bottle mass is editable because published figures vary: about 9.3–9.9 g for a modern 0.5 L bottle by industry averages, with an NIH-cited range of 13–26 g across markets. No default prices are presented as facts — everything money-related comes from what you type.
