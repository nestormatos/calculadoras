Printable Water Tracker

Prefer pen and paper? Generate a weekly check-off sheet sized to your glass, print it, and stick it on the fridge.

Configure your sheet

One page, seven days, one box per glass — sized to your bottle or glass.

Settings are saved in this browser — nothing is sent anywhere.

Weekly Water Tracker

How the sheet is sized

Grid size

Columns = ceil(goal ÷ glass size), capped at 12
Rows = 7 days of the current week

Totals row

Each box = one glass (e.g. 8 fl oz)
Full row ≈ your daily goal in fl oz

Prefer tracking on screen? The Daily Water Tracker logs every glass for you, the Bottle Count tool converts your goal into bottle refills, and the Progress Chart graphs the week.

Disclaimer

This sheet is an organizational aid for general wellness, not medical advice. If you are pregnant, nursing, or manage a condition affecting fluid balance (kidney, heart or liver disease), consult a healthcare professional about your daily target.
