How the estimate is built

Base formula (by weight)

Imperial: weight (lb) × 0.50 = fl oz/day
Metric: weight (kg) × 1.10 = fl oz/day

Adjustments

Activity: ×1.0 / ×1.1 / ×1.2 / ×1.3
Climate: ×1.0 / ×1.15 / ×1.3

The base follows the widely used "half your body weight in ounces" guideline; activity and climate multipliers reflect common sports-medicine practice of increasing fluids with sweat losses and heat exposure. For weight-only precision, use the Water Intake by Weight tool.
