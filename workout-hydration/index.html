How the plan is built

Sweat loss estimate

Sweat rate (fl oz/h) ≈ 0.34 × weight (kg) × intensity × climate
Intensity: ×0.7 / ×1.0 / ×1.3 / ×1.6
Climate: ×0.85 / ×1.0 / ×1.25

Before · During · After

Before: 0.2 fl oz × kg, about 2 hours prior
During: sweat rate ÷ 3, sipped every 15–20 min
After: 135% of sweat lost (guideline 125–150%), within 2h

The 0.34 fl oz/kg/hour baseline reflects typical sweat losses at moderate effort reported in sports-medicine research. Pre-loading with 0.2 fl oz/kg about two hours before exercise and replacing 125–150% of losses afterward follow widely used position-stand guidance. Sweat rates vary widely between people, so treat these numbers as a starting point and refine them with real before-and-after weigh-ins.

Disclaimer

This calculator provides general wellness estimates for healthy adults and is not medical advice. Fluid needs vary with fitness, heat acclimatization, altitude and health status. If you have a heart, kidney or liver condition, or you train in extreme heat, consult a healthcare professional or sports-medicine specialist.
