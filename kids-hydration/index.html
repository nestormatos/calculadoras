Practical tips for parents

  • Keep a filled bottle within reach — kids drink more when water is visible.
  • Offer water with every meal and snack, not just when they ask.
  • Send a labeled bottle to school and ask about refill breaks.
  • On sport days, encourage a few sips every 15–20 minutes.
  • Water and milk are the best everyday choices; limit sugary drinks.

These values follow Institute of Medicine (National Academies) adequate-intake levels for total water — everything a child drinks plus the water in food. Roughly one fifth of intake normally comes from food, which is why the drinks target sits near 75% of the total. Active play and hot weather raise needs, so the activity multiplier nudges the target up on busier days.
