How the schedule is built

Even split

per drink = goal ÷ drinks,
rounded to nearest 0.5 fl oz;
final drink takes the remainder

Timing

window = wake-up → bedtime − 30 min
interval = window ÷ (drinks − 1)
times rounded to nearest 5 min

Spacing intake evenly keeps hydration steady instead of flooding your system all at once, and stopping 30 minutes before bed reduces night-time bathroom trips. Amounts are rounded to the nearest 0.5 fl oz so they're easy to pour; the last drink absorbs any rounding difference so the day still totals your exact goal.
