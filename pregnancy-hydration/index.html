How the target is built

EFSA (Europe)

total water: 68 fl oz baseline
pregnancy: +10 fl oz · breastfeeding: +24 fl oz
drinks = total × 0.80 · food = total × 0.20
glasses = drinks ÷ 8 fl oz

IOM (United States) — table values

reference: 91 total / 74 drinks
pregnancy: 101 total / 78 drinks
breastfeeding: 128 total / 105 drinks
(fl oz/day; food = total − drinks)

According to EFSA, adequate total water intake for women is 68 fl oz/day, rising by 10 fl oz in pregnancy and by 24 fl oz while breastfeeding; as a practical guide, about 80% comes from drinks and 20% from food, and one glass is taken as 8 fl oz. The IOM (US National Academies) publishes its own adequate-intake values, shown above. During pregnancy, keep caffeine under 200 mg per day — the Alcohol & Caffeine guide shows what that means in cups. Heat and exercise raise needs on their own — use the Hot Weather and Workout Hydration tools for that instead of inflating this target.

Disclaimer

General guidance for healthy people — not medical advice. If you have kidney, heart or liver disease, or you have been told to restrict fluids or sodium, follow your doctor's instructions. If you have severe nausea or vomiting in pregnancy (hyperemesis gravidarum) or preeclampsia, your fluid needs are individual — follow your doctor's instructions.
