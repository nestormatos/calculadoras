Conversion reference

Base unit

All conversions pass through US fluid ounces:
1 L = 33.8140 fl oz
1 cup = 8 fl oz

Household measures

1 glass = 8.5 fl oz
1 bottle = 16.9 fl oz

Ounces here are US fluid ounces; the imperial (UK) fluid ounce is slightly larger. Glass and bottle sizes are common reference values — check your own bottle's label, and use the Bottle Size Recommender if you're picking one.
