How the count is calculated

Exact division

bottles needed = daily goal ÷ bottle size
shown to 1 decimal place

Whole-bottle plan

full bottles = floor(goal ÷ size)
top-up = goal − full bottles × size
rounded-up count = ceil(goal ÷ size)

The plan always rounds up: the rounded-up bottle count covers at least 100% of your goal, so you never finish the day short. The final bottle is usually a partial pour — the "leftover top-up" is exactly what remains after the full bottles.
