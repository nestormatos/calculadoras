How the estimate is built

Sweat & sodium loss

sweat loss (L) = sweat rate × duration
sodium loss (mg) = sweat loss × sweat sodium (mg/L)
salt equivalent (g) = sodium (mg) ÷ 393.4

Replacement from drinks

drink sodium target: 500–700 mg/L (ACSM)
sodium in drinks = drink volume × 500–700
replaced % = drink sodium ÷ sodium loss × 100

Studies show sweat sodium typically ranges from about 200 mg/L to over 2,000 mg/L; the Low, Average and High profiles (460 / 920 / 1,380 mg/L) are practical estimates within that range. According to the American College of Sports Medicine (ACSM), drinks for prolonged exercise should provide roughly 500–700 mg of sodium per liter. As a practical guide, table salt is 39.34% sodium by weight — that is the ratio behind the salt equivalent (1 g of sodium ≈ 2.54 g of salt).

Disclaimer

General guidance for healthy people — not medical advice. If you have kidney, heart or liver disease, or you have been told to restrict fluids or sodium, follow your doctor's instructions. Drinking too much can also be dangerous: it can lower blood sodium (hyponatremia). This tool is not intended for people on low-sodium diets (for example for hypertension, kidney or heart disease).
