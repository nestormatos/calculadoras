How the estimate is built

Base intake (by weight)

Imperial: weight (lb) × 0.50 = fl oz/day
Metric: weight (kg) × 1.10 = fl oz/day

Heat & exposure adjustments

Heat factor: <77°F ×1.0 · 77–90°F ×1.15 · 90–100°F ×1.3 · >100°F ×1.45
(thresholds: 25 / 32 / 38 °C)
Extra: 12 fl oz × outdoor hours × activity (×1.0–1.8)

The base follows the widely used "half your body weight in ounces" guideline. The heat tiers reflect how sweat losses climb as air temperature passes roughly 25 °C (77 °F), and the 12 fl oz per outdoor hour approximates the additional losses seen with heat exposure, scaled by how hard you are working. Humidity pushes losses higher still — when the air is sticky, aim for the upper end.

Disclaimer

This calculator provides general wellness estimates only and is not medical advice. High heat carries a real risk of heat exhaustion and heat stroke — learn the warning signs (dizziness, headache, nausea, confusion) and seek help if they appear. Older adults, young children and people with heart, kidney or liver conditions should consult a healthcare professional about fluid needs in hot weather.
