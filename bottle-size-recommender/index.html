How the recommendation works

Sizing logic

Ideal capacity = daily goal ÷ refills
Snap up to: 11 · 17 · 20 · 25 · 34 · 41 · 51 · 68 fl oz

Carry-style limits

Running handheld: max 25 fl oz
Car cup holder: max 34 fl oz
Backpack side pocket: max 117 fl oz
If capped, the refill count rises to keep your goal

Snapping up to a common size gives you headroom instead of a shortfall: a slightly larger bottle means you can always finish the day ahead of target. The carry limits reflect what realistically fits — a running hand strap, a standard cup holder or a backpack pocket — and when the ideal size exceeds that limit, the tool keeps the smaller bottle and adds the extra refill you would need.

Disclaimer

Recommendations are practical suggestions based on common bottle sizes, not medical advice. Your daily fluid target itself should come from a guideline-based tool or a healthcare professional, especially if you manage a condition that affects fluid balance.
