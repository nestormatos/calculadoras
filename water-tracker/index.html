    How tracking works

    Progress & remaining

    Progress % = total today ÷ goal × 100
    Remaining = max(0, goal − total)

    Streak rule

    Streak = consecutive days with total ≥ goal
    (today counts once the goal is met)

    Entries are stored only in this browser under dated keys, so the Hydration Progress Chart can read the same history. Pace yourself with the Drinking Schedule, set nudges with the Water Reminder, or prefer paper with the Printable Water Tracker.
