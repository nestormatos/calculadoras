Frequently asked questions

Are these tools really free?

Yes. Every tool is free to use, with no account, no subscription and no usage limits.

Is my data private?

Everything runs in your browser. Tracker logs and challenge progress are stored only in your device's local storage — nothing is sent to a server.

Which units do the calculators support?

Both imperial (pounds, fluid ounces, cups) and metric (kilograms, liters). Most tools let you switch systems at any time without losing your input.

Are the results medical advice?

No. The tools provide general wellness estimates based on widely cited guidelines (Mayo Clinic, WHO, CDC). Individual needs vary; consult a healthcare professional for personal advice, especially if you have a medical condition.

Disclaimer

These tools are for general wellness and informational purposes only and do not constitute medical advice. If you are pregnant, nursing, an elite athlete, or manage a condition affecting fluid balance (kidney, heart or liver disease), consult a qualified healthcare professional before changing your fluid intake.
