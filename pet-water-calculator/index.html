How the estimate is built

Maintenance formulas (per species)

Dog: 4.5 × weight(kg)^0.75 = fl oz/day
Cat: 2.7 × weight(kg)^0.75 = fl oz/day

Food moisture & drinking target

Water from food = food(g) × moisture%
To drink = total − water from food (min 0)
Typical range = to drink × 0.8 – 1.2

The maintenance formulas come from the clinical maintenance rates used in the AAHA Fluid Therapy Guidelines, applied here to a healthy adult animal at rest. According to AAHA, total water needs include moisture from food, so we subtract what the diet already provides. Real intake varies with activity, weather and individual habit, which is why the result is presented as a ±20% range rather than a single target. Moisture defaults (dry 10%, wet 78%) are typical label values — always prefer the figure printed on your pet's food.

Disclaimer

This calculator provides a general estimate for healthy adult dogs and cats only. It is not intended for puppies, kittens, pregnant or nursing mothers, or sick animals — in those cases, consult your veterinarian. This tool is not veterinary advice and never replaces professional care.
